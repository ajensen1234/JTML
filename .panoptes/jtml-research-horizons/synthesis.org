* Executive Summary

The six angles answer the question — "what are the next layers of deeper exploration?" — with a converging, and mildly surprising, picture: the highest-leverage next work is **neither a new algorithm nor a new architecture — it is measurement infrastructure**. The repo's two most valuable assets are also its two biggest blind spots. The oracle (the R2-validated, IoU-gated golden-master, "a rare asset" per angle 02) gates *one* case — Kneel_1 femur, 1 of 3 frames, monoplane, a single trunk stage at a flat ad-hoc budget of 3000 — which means the leaf z-refinement stage, the one stage whose entire purpose is the repo's documented weak axis (6.31 mm z_trans gap), has never been exercised by any test (angle 06). And the cost path that gate protects — the R15 load-bearing DIRECT_DILATION family — contains seven documented live bugs with zero metric-math test coverage (angles 01 + 05). Every other research direction's verdict must land on instruments that do not yet exist.

**The highest-leverage single direction: make the z weak axis measurable in production shape.** All six angles converge on out-of-plane translation as the universal weak axis: Penney 1998 excludes depth from the sub-0.54 mm/deg claim even for the best metrics (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9845314/), Yamazaki 2004 says depth accuracy "is relatively poor because a change in the depth position causes only small changes in the 2-D silhouette" (https://researchr.org/publication/YamazakiWNSTYT04), DAMN's learned regressor reports 25 mm out-of-plane vs 0.75 mm in-plane (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0270596), Jensen 2023's validated DIRECT-JTA lineage reports 2.6 mm ML as its largest error (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.00057), and the repo's own measured z gap sits at 6.31 mm against a 15 mm informational tolerance (test/golden/baseline.json:53,61). Concretely: (a) add a production-shaped multi-stage oracle variant (cumulative 20k→25k→30k→35k, dilation 6/4/1 or 6/3/1 pending the contradiction below, z opened to ±15 in leaf, tibia-after-femur, sym_trap disambiguation) so the weak axis is observed through the real stage loop (angle 06), and (b) build the metric-ablation harness with its z-profile probe — sweep z across ±15 mm at truth pose and plot the cost curve per variant (angle 01 calls this "the single most informative new experiment"). Together these convert the repo's biggest unknown into a measured quantity, and they make every later decision (cost-variant ranking, weight calibration, dilation-schedule ablation, DIRECT-variant verdicts, ML initializer quality) adjudicable.

The algorithm-improvement work then becomes cheap and evidence-gated. The DIRECT core (src/domain/direct_optimizer.cpp) is a faithful Jones-1993 with effectively epsilon=0 — the one configuration the 25-years survey documents as pathological for expensive costs, because it over-refines suboptimal local minima and can burn much of the 20k trunk budget before the branch stage sees fresh dilation (angle 02, https://websites.umich.edu/~mdolaboratory/pdf/Jones2020a.pdf). The highest-evidence, lowest-risk fix is refinement control (epsilon / delta-limit / DIRECT-restart's adaptive epsilon), then the DIRECT-l infinity-norm size measure, then two-step Pareto (DIRECT-GL) — each gated by a headless analytic battery before any GPU spend, and by the oracle afterward. On the cost side, the chamfer (DistanceMapMetric) is the only z-sensitive term in the default cost and its blend weight is accidental; the geometric priors at weight 1 are ~0.01–0.3% of the base cost — effectively inert — and sym_trap's 75/500 weights are the only ones in the right scale (angle 01). The repo's documented deviation from the DIRECT paper (dilating *both* sides of the comparison vs the paper's dilation-pyramid-on-fluoro-edge) has never been ablated — and one live bug (bug-4, the always-Trunk stage guard) currently makes the paper-style path unreachable (angle 01).

The architectural verdicts are equally clear. **QML: NO-GO now** — it is a plan-005-scale rewrite (Path A) whose payoff is styling, none of the current problems are QML problems, the ecosystem is QWidgets-first (ParaView, 3D Slicer, MITK, ImFusion), and the Slicer maintainer's warning ("plan extra time for testing and development at each Qt or VTK release") is a small-lab cost the repo cannot absorb quietly; the correct action is a one-line decision record replacing the "QML-swappable later" comment (angle 04). **Compute: sync-bound, not kernel-bound** — ~15 kernel launches and ~5 synchronous D2H pipeline drains per evaluation, ~175k drains per 35k-eval frame; measure first with the already-wired nvtx dep, then remove per-eval syncs (angle 05). **Determinism is better than the docs claim**: every reduction in the DIRECT_DILATION path is int-atomic (order-independent, value-deterministic); only the DRR float atomicAdd (never called by the cost path) is order-nondeterministic — so the 6.31 mm gap is cross-machine float/FMA divergence + DIRECT chaos + a real production-vs-oracle backface-culling config difference, not atomic ordering (angle 05). **The ML pathway's right shape is segmentation→initializer→DIRECT** — the repo's DIRECT stage is verbatim the published, independently validated stage 3 of the lab's own Jensen 2023 pipeline (0.8/0.5/2.6 mm and 0.5–1.7° vs RSA/mocap); ML should be invested in as an *initializer* with renderer-generated synthetic labels (no clinical annotation needed), never as an end-to-end pose regressor, and the oracle must gate the *refined* pose, not raw estimator output (angle 03).

Recommended course of action, in order: (1) fix the seven live bugs and delete the dead duplicate chamfer term with pin-first tests — days of work, unpoisons every downstream measurement (angles 01, 05); (2) build the metric-ablation harness with the z-profile probe, headless first (angle 01); (3) add the production-shaped multi-stage oracle variant, calibrate the IoU threshold to a cross-GPU noise floor, and wire a self-hosted gpu-labeled CI runner (angle 06) — the highest-leverage single direction; (4) then run the DIRECT variant switch and the cost-family z remedies, each oracle-adjudicated (angles 02, 01); (5) sequence the ML initializer pathway after the oracle can measure refined poses, and expand fixtures toward tibia and biplane for the z ceiling (angles 03, 06); (6) defer QML, and write the intended-purpose statement + SOUP inventory now — regulatory posture is a written decision, and AGPL is not a shield (angle 06).

* Key Findings

** The z weak axis is the load-bearing research problem — and the current oracle cannot see it
Consolidated from angles 01, 02, 03, 06. Convergence: five independent sources document monoplane depth as the hardest axis for every method family — Penney 1998 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9845314/), Yamazaki 2004 (https://researchr.org/publication/YamazakiWNSTYT04), DAMN's 25 mm out-of-plane vs 0.75 mm in-plane (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0270596), Jensen 2023's 2.6 mm ML (largest DOF error) (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.00057), Veriserum's dual-plane motivation ("superior out-of-plane accuracy", https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05483). The repo-side mechanism is geometric: a ±15 mm z shift at ~1.1 m source distance changes magnification <1%, far inside the 6 px dilation tolerance, so dilated overlap is nearly flat in z (angle 01, derived from baseline.json + assumed SID, medium confidence on the arithmetic). Compounding: the leaf stage exists precisely to refine z (Z_SEARCH_RANGE z=15, settings_constants.h), yet the oracle runs a single trunk stage at flat budget 3000 with flat dilation 6 — so the weak axis's one dedicated refinement stage is untested (angle 06). The repo's own z estimator (area-ratio z = -pd·sqrt(sum_proj/sum_seg), src/services/implant_estimator.cpp) is a deterministic readout of the same weak signal; expecting any method — learned or classical — to fix monoplane z is unrealistic (angles 01, 03, 06 agree). The actionable consequence: measure the z-profile per cost variant in the harness, treat z > ~3 mm as a biplane/multi-view problem, and design TRE targets as in-plane sub-mm / out-of-plane ≤1.5–2 mm (angles 01, 06; biplane field accuracy 0.01±0.65°/0.59 mm per Giphart, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23021610/).

** The R15 load-bearing cost path contains 7 live bugs, zero metric-math tests, and an uncalibrated blend — it is the foundation that must be cleaned first
Consolidated from angles 01 and 05. Angle 01 found in the registered variants: bug-1 uninitialized `min_dist` read (UB) in DD_NEW_POLE_CONSTRAINT (src/compute/DD_NEW_POLE_CONSTRAINT.cpp:155-169); bug-2 Y_dist ≡ X_dist copy-paste making the Y_TRANS constraint a no-op (141-148 vs 133-139); bug-3 sym_trap builds the tibia transform with x replaced by z, corrupting varus/valgus and pole distance (src/compute/sym_trap_function.cpp:104-108); bug-4 the always-true stage guard in CostFunctionManager makes DIRECT_DILATION_T1's non-trunk path unreachable (src/compute/CostFunctionManager.cpp:63-64). Angle 05 found in the active cost path: DistanceMapMetric_Kernel's broken index formula (`blockIdx.y + gridDim.x + blockIdx.x` instead of `blockIdx.y * gridDim.x`, src/compute/distance_map_metric.cu:20) — a subtle bias in the trunk cost every evaluation; ImplantMahfouzMetric's pointer-vs-value denominator check (`if (pixel_score_ != 0)` — always true, so a zero denominator divides by zero, src/compute/implant_mahfouz_metric.cu:346,464) plus a double→int atomicAdd truncation; and CurvatureHeatmapMetric as a stub that returns 0 without launching its kernel (curvature_hausdorf_metric.cu:41-76). Test coverage: test/unit/test_cost_function.cpp covers only the parameter registry — zero metric math (angle 01). The metrics also mutate the rendered image in place, so composition only works by mutation ordering (angle 05). The documented "DIFFERENT FROM JTA PAPER" symmetric-dilation deviation (5 cost files) is untested by design decision, not evidence (angle 01). The scale finding compounds everything: overlap terms are O(10³–10⁴) while the chamfer mean is O(1–10) and the SAME_Z/pole priors at weight 1 are ~0.01–0.3% of base cost — the blend weights are accidental, and sym_trap's 75/500 are the only calibrated ones (angle 01).

** The DIRECT core is faithful-but-unmodernized; refinement control — not a new algorithm — is the highest-evidence optimizer upgrade
Angle 02 (with refs/ Jones 1993 read): the repo implements Jones-1993's lower-right convex hull with effectively epsilon=0, L2 size measure, single-largest-denormalized-side trisection, no Lipschitz estimation, no restart, no local refinement. The 25-years survey (https://websites.umich.edu/~mdolaboratory/pdf/Jones2020a.pdf) documents epsilon=0's excessive-local-refinement pathology (Shubert: >10,000 evals at 0.01% accuracy with epsilon=0 vs 2933 with 1e-4), and the 2022 study's headline for expensive costs: "when objective functions are expensive, local refinement reduction techniques improve performance" (https://epublications.vu.lt/object/elaba:143490244/143490244.pdf) — the GPU-DRR case exactly. Dimensionality is on the repo's side (6D is DIRECT's sweet spot; deterministic algorithms excel in low dimensions, https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05759). Compounding with angle 05: the per-eval cost is sync-dominated (~5 D2H drains/eval), so every evaluation is expensive in wall-clock terms — the precise regime where refinement control pays. Compounding with angle 03: a good ML initializer narrows the search box, directly mitigating the same over-refinement risk. The trunk→2×branch→leaf stage design is the documented multistage/zoom family pattern (Jones-2001 revision, MrDIRECT, HD-DIRECT, DIRECT-JTA itself) and is well-posed *provided* each stage's budget is not squandered (angle 02). The one configuration deviation with no literature counterpart — splitting by largest *denormalized* side — is an implicit dimension weighting (z-heavy) that should be kept and documented (angle 02; NLopt NOSCAL is the only analogue). Noisy-DIRECT (plural sampling) is explicitly out of scope at this budget (angle 02) — and angle 05 confirms the cost is deterministic per machine, so stochastic machinery is moot.

** Determinism is better than the docs claim — and the doc contradiction is itself a finding
Angle 05: every reduction in the DIRECT_DILATION path (WhitePixelSum, difference kernel, distance-map, IoU, L1,1, Mahfouz) uses int atomics — integer addition is order-independent, so these are value-deterministic run-to-run on a machine. The only order-nondeterministic production reduction is the DRR fill's float atomicAdd (src/compute/render_drr_engine.cu:328-352), which the DIRECT cost path never calls. This directly contradicts the testability doc's claim that "CUDA reductions/atomics are not bit-reproducible" (docs/solutions/conventions/jtml-testability-and-cmake-conventions-2026-08-07.md §6) — the tolerance-based gate philosophy is correct, the stated mechanism is not. The 6.31 mm z gap is better explained by (1) float FMA/arch divergence (CMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=native makes binaries machine-specific) amplified by DIRECT's discrete trisect decisions, and (2) a real config divergence: production runs backface culling ON (src/coordinator/optimizer_manager.cpp:716-722) while the oracle runs it OFF "to match label generator Study2Grid" (test/oracle/oracle_test.cpp:190-197) — the silhouette itself differs between production and gate. Compounding with angle 06: the IoU>0.85 threshold sits at measured 0.9936 with no noise-floor calibration, and VMAF-style practice says golden thresholds must be sized to the metric's own noise; the fix is a cross-GPU noise-floor measurement (RTX 3090 + one other) plus margin, and promoting per-px L1 (0.0207) to a banded secondary gate. This resolves angle 02's open question (is the GPU cost stochastic?) — it is deterministic per machine; refinement control + oracle gating is the right response, not noisy-DIRECT.

** The oracle is both crown jewel and single-point blind spot; its expansion is the cheapest way to make everything else measurable
Angle 06: golden-master/characterization testing is the correct frame (behavior-preservation, not correctness — R1/R2 aligned), but coverage is one fixture with unknown gate sensitivity, an ad-hoc budget reduction (3000 vs production 20k→35k), a 3600 s TIMEOUT, no standalone gpu label, and no GPU CI. The production vs oracle stage-shape divergence means a regression in SetCallOffset bookkeeping, dilation scheduling, or tibial-ambiguity handling would pass the oracle today. Angle 02 independently calls the IoU>0.85 appearance gate "a legitimate, rare asset... the right arbiter for GPU-tier behavior" — satisfying R2 anti-circularity. Compounding: angle 03 notes the gate is a silhouette-space proxy that cannot distinguish a 2 mm in-plane miss from a 15 mm z miss; the field convention is per-DoF pose-space RMS with independent ground truth, and the lab's own lineage paper (Jensen 2023, https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.00057) already defines the reporting template (per-DoF RMS + bias + blunder rate, tibial ambiguous zone <3.6°). The multi-stage oracle variant is also the acceptance test for the R15 shared stage-loop refactor, and Moré & Wild data profiles (https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/080724083) give a principled way to pick a fast CI budget shape (target <15 min, IoU≈0.98) instead of the ad-hoc 3000. Oracle-quality testing itself is a recognized discipline (checked coverage, https://www.st.cs.uni-saarland.de/publications/files/schuler-stvr-2013.pdf): a perturbation suite (perturb pose by the 6.31 mm z gap and ±1 mm/±1°, break the dilation schedule, swap the cost variant — assert the gate trips) measures whether the gate would catch a real regression.

** The ML pathway's right shape is segmentation → initializer → DIRECT; the repo already implements the validated stage 3 — and synthetic labels are the unlock, not clinical data
Angle 03: three independent groups (UF/Denver, ETH, LUMC) converge on segmentation-first + optimization refinement; end-to-end regression alone has a hard ceiling (DAMN 25 mm out-of-plane at 50%; Joint Track ML's NFD init is "within a few millimeters or degrees" and DIRECT brings it to sub-mm). The repo's optimizer_manager + DIRECT_DILATION default is verbatim the published, independently validated DIRECT-JTA stage 3 (Jensen et al., J Arthroplasty 2023, https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.00057): trunk/branch/leaf, L1 cost on dilated contour, decreasing dilation; external validation 0.8/0.5/2.6 mm and 0.5–1.7° vs RSA/mocap — "the classical path is the defensible one today," and it is the repo's strongest evidence asset. Training labels are generated synthetically from known poses via model rendering in every successful pipeline (Joint Track ML: 6,284 train images per network; DAMN; DeepDRR; Veriserum 110,990 dual-plane images, https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05483) — no clinical annotation needed, and the repo's renderer + Study2Grid already fit the recipe. Compounding with angle 02: an initializer narrows DIRECT's search, directly mitigating the epsilon=0 over-refinement budget risk. Compounding with angle 05: the torch-in-services layer change (004 U8) is real but the actual perf bug is CUDACachingAllocator::emptyCache() per frame (src/services/segmentation_controller.cpp:25-27), which defeats the caching allocator. Compounding with angle 06: models are implant-geometry-specific (DAMN: per-combination retraining) — each .pt must pair with metadata (implant design, pose distribution, input size), and the oracle must gate the *refined* pose with per-DoF gates split in-plane/out-of-plane (field convention), not raw estimator output. The learned-registration frontier (DiffPose/xvr) requires CT volumes — a different product than the STL-based pipeline; the nearest learned-refinement opportunity is a differentiable silhouette renderer à la Veriserum (MAE < 0.8 mm / 0.9°), worth only after the segmentation pathway is live (angle 03).

** Compute is sync-bound, not kernel-bound; the fix is per-eval sync removal, not kernel rewrites
Angle 05 (from source reading): one DIRECT_DILATION monoplane eval = ~15 kernel launches + ~5 synchronous D2H cudaMemcpy's (2 in Render, 1 in FastImplantDilationMetric, 2 in DistanceMapMetric); 35,000 evals/frame → ~175k host-GPU round-trips/frame, on the order of seconds of pure serialization. All allocations are hoisted out of the hot path (RenderEngine::InitializeCUDA, GPUMetrics ctor) — the churn is per-frame (GPUImage::UploadImageToGPU reallocates on every upload; emptyCache after every torch frame), not per-eval. The two Render() D2H syncs exist only so the host can decide the fill launch size and check overflow — replaceable by a device-side upper bound + one copy-back per stage (angle 05 rec 2, "the single highest-leverage kernel-level change"). The metrics mutate the rendered image in place (composition works only via mutation ordering) — a scratch buffer makes the metric a pure function and unblocks caching, reuse, and CPU fallback. The in-app "ms/call" meter divides clock() (process CPU time) by call count — it structurally excludes GPU time (optimizer_manager.cpp:1274-1281). nvtx is declared in pixi.toml and LIBNVTOOLSEXT is wired, but there is zero instrumentation in source — the measure-first step is a 30-minute Nsight Systems capture (angle 05). A CPU fallback (host already computes Canny/dilate/distance maps in OpenCV; render is a software rasterizer) is "trivially portable" and would make the cost pipeline runnable on non-GPU CI (angle 05 rec 9) — compounding with angle 01's headless Tier-0 ablation harness and angle 02's headless analytic battery.

** QML is a NO-GO now; the seams are the real asset, and the decision should be recorded, not deferred
Angle 04 (with repo-local VTK 9.3.0 source read): the pinned VTK build already compiles and installs libvtkGUISupportQtQuick-9.3.so (unlinked by anything), and QQuickVTKItem is the non-deprecated API — but a full QML front-end is a plan-005-scale project: ~1,600 lines of view content replaced (ArrangeMainScreenLayout alone ~675 lines, src/view/mainscreen.cpp:375-~1050), every QItemSelectionModel call site re-plumbed (30+ sites; QML ListView has no selectionModel property — Qt's ItemSelectionModel only works with TableView), and interactor.h's file-scope globals reworked into QQuickVTKItem's render-thread ownership model ("All VTK objects are owned by and run on the QML render thread", verbatim from _deps/vtk/source/GUISupport/QtQuick/QQuickVTKItem.h). The ecosystem is unanimous: ParaView and 3D Slicer (the two flagship VTK apps) are QWidgets-first, as are MITK and ImFusion's QtDisplayWidget; the Slicer maintainer's verbatim advice is "you may need to invest time into making the VTK renderer work in QML and plan extra time for testing and development at each Qt or VTK release" (https://discourse.vtk.org/t/vtk-9-pyqt-application/5079). The bug tail touches exactly JTML's surface: mouse-position/DPR mis-translation, draw-pick interactor styles (vtkInteractorStyleRubberBandPick family) failing, vtkResliceImageViewer broken. The xcb-only constraint is inherited, not fixed, by QML. What survives untouched: FrameListModel/ModelListModel (QAbstractListModel is the QML-native interface), SessionState, all services/coordinator/domain layers — the plan-004 seams are genuinely QML-ready (which is why the correct move is a one-line decision record, not a migration). Path B (QQuickWidget islands) is the bounded hedge if a QML showcase is ever needed; the render-regression contract (widget->grab() under forced xcb) is the hidden cost of any QML path (no grab() equivalent; offscreen testing moves to QQuickRenderControl) (angle 04).

** Accuracy evidence for validation exists in the repo's own published lineage — the oracle is verification, not validation
Angle 06: the oracle is a verification artifact (IEC 62304 "did we build it right"); accuracy evidence ("did we build the right thing") is a separate ladder the lineage paper already climbed: in-silico known-pose recovery → sawbones phantom + mocap (45 images, 0.72/0.31/1.82 mm, 0.56/0.63/0.84°) → cadaver marker gold standard (Giphart: 0.01±0.65°/0.59 mm, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23021610/) → clinical vs human-supervised/RSA (113 image pairs: 0.8/0.5/2.6 mm, 1.0/1.2/1.7°) (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.00057). Compounding with angle 03: these per-DoF numbers are also the reporting template and the accuracy bar the tool "would need to reproduce" — and the credibility ladder is replicable at lab scale starting with synthetic poses (free via Study2Grid). Regulatory reality (angle 06): a 2D-3D registration tool that processes images fails the FDA CDS exclusion (21 USC 360j(o)(4) — software that processes/analyzes medical images is not excluded), MDR class IIa at minimum if it informs individual-patient decisions, and "the regulatory requirements do not depend on whether software is placed on the market as open source" (https://blog.johner-institute.com/regulatory-affairs/open-source-software-as-a-medical-device/) — so "research only" is a written intended-purpose statement, not the AGPL. A SOUP inventory (VTK, torch, OpenCV, Eigen, Qt, hegel) is cheap to write now and is required content for any IEC 62304/MDR Annex II package later.

* Contradictions and Open Questions

- **Dilation schedule: 6/3/1 vs 6/4/1 (unresolved).** golden_oracle.org prose and angles 01/03 describe trunk 6 / branch 3 / leaf 1; angle 06 derives 6/4/1 from settings_constants.h (TRUNK_DILATION 6, BRANCH_DILATION_DECREASE 2, Z_SEARCH_DILATION 1) and notes the runtime value is read from CostFunctionManager parameters. R15 calls the dilation design "load-bearing" — the authoritative value must be pinned by reading runtime values (a small headless probe) before any ablation of the DIFFERENT-FROM-JTA deviation is trusted.
- **Docs vs code on CUDA determinism.** The testability doc (§6) claims "CUDA reductions/atomics are not bit-reproducible"; angle 05's int-atomic audit shows the DIRECT_DILATION path is value-deterministic per machine. The tolerance-gate philosophy survives; the mechanism is wrong. Decide: update the doc, and treat the doc as evidence of an unresolved mental model.
- **Backface-culling config divergence.** Production runs backface culling ON (optimizer_manager.cpp:716-722); the oracle runs OFF "to match Study2Grid" (oracle_test.cpp:190-197). Either the production silhouette legitimately differs from the label generator's (document why), or align them — the divergence sits inside the load-bearing gate (angle 05).
- **Per-variant z-ranking is a hypothesis, not a measurement.** Angle 01's expected ranking (chamfer-heavy DIRECT_DILATION ≥ Mahfouz ≥ pure overlap) and the "priors at weight 1 are inert" claim rest on metric semantics + scale arithmetic; only the z-profile probe can confirm. Similarly, the 6.31 mm gap's attribution (FMA divergence vs DIRECT chaos vs backface config) is unfalsified from source alone — an A/B isolates it (angle 05).
- **DIRECT-GL's 6D caution.** The two-step Pareto selection directly targets the weak axis (local step = nondominated on distance-to-current-best) but its documented Hartman-6 regression (8793 vs 571 evals) is a red flag for 6D; it must earn its place on the analytic battery before GPU spend (angle 02). Also open: whether DIRECT-l's infinity-norm size measure helps specifically on the z-anisotropic 6D pose cost (no published analogue).
- **Is the GPU cost noisy?** Angle 02 flagged DIRECT-S/Noisy-DIRECT as conditional on run-to-run stochasticity; angle 05 answers: deterministic per machine (int atomics), cross-machine float divergence only. The residual open item: whether cuDNN/cuBLAS algorithm selection adds nondeterminism to the ML path (never queried/set in-repo, angle 05) — irrelevant to DIRECT but relevant to ML-oracle reproducibility.
- **Estimator validity (area-ratio z + phaseCorrelate + Euler recovery).** No public provenance found for this exact estimator; phase correlation is a translation-only method (degrades under scale/orientation mismatch), the area-ratio z assumes 1/z² area scaling with a silently-saturating clamp, and Euler targets have documented degeneracies the community avoids with 6D/se(3) heads (angle 03). It is unvalidated on synthetic poses — cheap to test, risky to trust as a DIRECT initializer.
- **Oracle gate sensitivity unknown.** IoU threshold 0.85 vs measured 0.9936 — no perturbation suite exists to show what would trip the gate (angles 06, 01). Threshold should be calibrated to a measured cross-GPU noise floor (angle 06).
- **Stale documentation in the repo itself.** golden_oracle.org budget prose (10k/stage) contradicts the authoritative 20k/2x5k/5k (local-recon, angle 02); golden_oracle.org's dilation prose (6/3/1) may contradict settings_constants.h (6/4/1, angle 06). The oracle spec is load-bearing documentation and should be reconciled in the same pass as the multi-stage oracle variant.
- **Learned vs classical for z — no contradiction, but an open cost question.** All angles agree learned methods do not fix monoplane z (DAMN 25 mm). The open item is whether gradient correlation (angle 01's literature-backed modern metric) earns its DRR-wiring cost; the z-profile probe on the existing family should run first, and gradient metrics only if overlap+chamfer+priors prove insufficient.

* Per-Angle Summaries

** Angle 1: Cost-Function & Similarity Metric Landscape
The 7 registered cost variants are three ingredients (dilated-edge overlap, chamfer, geometric priors) plus one Mahfouz intensity term, not seven metric families; dilated overlap is nearly flat in z (the 6.31 mm gap's mechanism), the chamfer is the only z-sensitive term, the priors at weight 1 are inert, and the DIFFERENT-FROM-JTA symmetric-dilation deviation is untested. Found 4 live bugs (bugs 1-4) plus an always-unreachable T1 path, and confirmed the commented-out DistanceMapMetric is a dead duplicate safe to delete. Prescribes the metric-ablation harness with the z-profile probe as the missing decision tool, followed by re-weighted chamfer, symmetric IoU, curvature-weighted edges, and calibrated priors.
Full report: [[file:angles/01-cost-function-landscape.org]]

** Angle 2: DIRECT Optimizer Variants & Modern DIRECT Research
The repo's DIRECT is a faithful JTA-flavored Jones-1993 with effectively epsilon=0 — the configuration the 25-years survey documents as pathological for expensive costs (over-refinement of suboptimal basins; the 20k trunk budget is the exposure). Highest-value, lowest-risk upgrades: epsilon/refinement control, the DIRECT-l infinity-norm size measure, two-step Pareto (DIRECT-GL, with a documented 6D caution), and keep the multistage trunk/branch/leaf as the well-posed zoom pattern. Prescribes a pure-seam Options-struct variant switch with a headless analytic battery (DIRECTGOLib/GKLS subsets) and the oracle IoU gate as independent Tier-2 ground truth; noisy-DIRECT explicitly out of scope.
Full report: [[file:angles/02-direct-variants.org]]

** Angle 3: ML Segmentation & Learned 2D-3D Registration
The community's proven architecture is segmentation-first + optimization refinement — and the repo's DIRECT stage is verbatim the published, independently validated stage 3 of the lab's own Jensen 2023 DIRECT-JTA pipeline (0.8–2.6 mm, 0.5–1.7° vs RSA/mocap). Learned regression alone has a hard ceiling (DAMN 25 mm out-of-plane); single-plane depth is the universal weak axis; training labels are generated synthetically via model rendering (no clinical annotation needed); models are implant-specific. Prescribes the ML pathway as an *initializer* for DIRECT, pose-space per-DoF gates, validating/replacing the area-ratio-z+phaseCorrelate estimator, and deferring differentiable rendering (CT-volume product).
Full report: [[file:angles/03-ml-2d3d-registration.org]]

** Angle 4: QML vs QWidgets Front-End Architecture
The pinned VTK 9.3 build already compiles QQuickVTKItem (unlinked), but a full QML front-end is a plan-005-scale rewrite: ~1,600 lines of view content, 30+ QItemSelectionModel call sites (QML ListView has no selectionModel), interactor.h globals reworked into a render-thread ownership model, and a documented bug tail touching exactly JTML's surface (DPR mouse picking, draw-pick styles). The ecosystem (ParaView, 3D Slicer, MITK, ImFusion) is QWidgets-first and maintainers warn of per-release QML+VTK testing costs. Verdict: NO-GO now; record the decision, keep the seams; Path B (QQuickWidget islands) is the bounded hedge; render-regression contract is the hidden cost of any QML path.
Full report: [[file:angles/04-qml-vs-widgets.org]]

** Angle 5: Compute Architecture & Performance Profiling
The merged jtml_compute SHARED lib is a single-stream, default-context pipeline: ~15 kernel launches + ~5 synchronous D2H copies per evaluation → ~175k pipeline drains per 35k-eval frame; sync-bound, not kernel-bound. Allocations are hoisted (churn is per-frame: upload reallocation, torch emptyCache-per-frame). Determinism: int-atomic reductions are value-deterministic; only the DRR float atomicAdd is order-nondeterministic (not in the cost path); the 6.31 mm gap is FMA/arch divergence + DIRECT chaos + a production-vs-oracle backface-culling divergence — contradicting the docs' "atomics not bit-reproducible" claim. Found 3 more live bugs (DistanceMapMetric index formula, Mahfouz pointer-vs-value guard, curvature stub) plus the in-place metric mutation contract. Prescribes nvtx/Nsight measurement first, per-eval sync removal, read-only metric scratch buffers, and a CPU fallback for headless parity.
Full report: [[file:angles/05-compute-architecture.org]]

** Angle 6: Oracle/Test Strategy at Scale & Clinical Validation
The two-tier oracle is exemplary golden-master practice but gates one case in a non-production shape (single trunk stage, flat budget 3000, backface OFF) — the leaf z-refinement stage is never exercised, so the 6.31 mm z gap's own refinement stage is untested. Highest-leverage moves: a production-shaped multi-stage oracle variant (cumulative 20k→35k, tibia-after-femur, sym_trap) as the R15 acceptance test; Moré & Wild data-profile budget selection; gate-sensitivity perturbation suite; cross-GPU threshold calibration; fixture expansion (frames, tibia, Subject0 biplane Labels + manifest, 2nd subject); and a separate accuracy-evidence ladder (synthetic → phantom → cadaver → clinical) replicating the lineage paper's per-DoF RMS table. Regulatory: intended-purpose decides device status (MDR/FDA — image processing fails the CDS exclusion), AGPL is not a shield; write the statement and SOUP inventory now.

## Papers shortlist (see papers/ recon for citations)

- DIRECT-GLh: hidden-constraint surrogate phi = f_min + d(x_min, x) (normalized) for infeasible points — keeps exploration at the feasibility boundary (relevant: unrenderable poses / NaN silhouette).
- 2022 study (12 variants): GL selection wins at large budgets (>= ~4k evals), IO wins small; 1-DTDV diagonal-vertex sampling dramatically best for boundary optima (2 evals vs 77-100); over-refinement restrictions HURT cheap functions (optimum ~0) — only -gb globally-biased helps expensive objectives.
- Laine 2011: fast silhouette-only software rasterizer patterns (coverage-only mode 20-30% faster, LUT masks, tile z-cull).
- Modular Primitives: differentiable rasterization primitives, occlusion-aware silhouette gradients, NDC depth z/w channel (z-axis signal!), analytic AA; inverse-rendering pose fitting at megapixel res — gradient polish after DIRECT global phase (thesis: ~12x fewer evals on engineering problems).

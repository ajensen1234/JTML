* Executive Summary

The owner's premise — "the current cost functions don't provide robust signal about the
symmetric axis" — is *half* true, and the synthesis of five independent angles says the
two halves require opposite treatments. For the **knee's** ill-conditioned DOFs
(varus/valgus, internal-external rotation, depth), the signal deficit is real and fixable
with signal engineering: two-sided intensity/attenuation + gradient-orientation features
are the documented rescue, and they are cheap to add in this codebase (a new render
target + a new cost variant, ~40–70 µs/eval, ~5–6k evals/s). For **hip/shoulder
continuous-symmetric parts** (cup spin about the polar axis, spherical-head rotation,
cylindrical-stem version), the missing signal is *provably absent from the projection
itself*: angle 01 proves from physics (rotation is measure-preserving, so every function
of a single-plane DRR — silhouette, intensity, gradient, curvature, learned — is exactly
invariant), angle 02 corroborates from the cup literature (D'Isidoro & Ferguson 2018:
"cannot be uniquely determined with the sole use of contours"; the field's
state-of-the-art ellipse methods parameterize the cup with 5 DOF and silently omit the
spin — Suh et al. 2025), and angle 05 confirms from geometry (cup C∞v, head O(3), stem
C1-near-axisymmetric). No cost function, no feature, no learned network can recover a DOF
that never enters the image. The cost-only framing is therefore the wrong frame for
symmetric parts.

The second headline: **the repo already has half the answer, shipped.** The domain
sym-trap directive — `CalculateSymTrap` 60-pose sweep, `compute_mirror_pose` Crane-Duffy
dyad, `tibial_pose_selector` (src/domain/sym_trap_functions.cpp,
ambiguous_pose_processing.cpp) — is exactly the Jensen-2024-style pattern for the
*discrete mirror* case (knee tibial tray), code-verified, and Jensen 2024's own numbers
show the analytic route is within a few points of the learned route (spline-only: 91.1%
accuracy vs 8 ML classifiers at 88–94%, which cost ~13,000 labeled sequences). Angle 04's
ML NO-GO verdict is upheld with a sharper reason: image-domain learning fails on
sim-to-real grounds (the repo's renderer is naive-DRR-class; even the *realistic*
simulators in the literature leave metal unmodeled — DeepDRR 2018), not merely on data
scale. What the repo lacks relative to the literature is not a better cost or a
classifier: it is (a) search-side symmetry tolerance, (b) sequence-level (temporal)
disambiguation, (c) ambiguity-as-data output, and (d) symmetry-group-aware machinery for
the *continuous* classes, which the dyad engine must not be retrofitted to.

The highest-leverage distinction, converged on by all five angles: **discrete symmetry
(enumerable ambiguity → post-processing fixes it; SymTrap works) vs continuous symmetry (a
1-parameter manifold of image-equivalent poses → an information floor that no silhouette
cost, and not even a biplane pair, can lift)**. Everything else in this synthesis hangs
off that axis: the DIRECT optimizer's documented budget-spreading over symmetric POH sets
(angle 03), the dyad's applicability boundary (angles 01/02/05), the curvature verdict
(angle 01), and the ML boundary (angle 04).

Recommended course of action, in order: **(1) Do the knee-side work now, on existing
seams** — un-stub `DirectOptimizer::Options::ties` (TieSelection::One; 0 extra evals; the
literature's documented fix for symmetric POH explosion), add a two-sided intensity/GO
cost variant (CostFunctionManager pattern; measure valley depth with the jtml.z_profile
instrument before wiring weights), emit ambiguity as pose-record data, complete the
pending curvature de-scope hygiene item, and add a sequence-level spline directive
(stage-script registry seam). **(2) Gate any hip/shoulder execution on what is missing** —
zero hip/shoulder/acetabular models exist in the repo and there is no symmetry probe; the
two declined layers (clinical DOF needs, measurement apparatus) are exactly the blockers,
so acquire models + build the rotation-profile probe first. **(3) Then build the
hip/shoulder-ready machinery** — symmetry-group dispatcher (domain layer), per-class pose
parameterization (cup 5-DOF with declared spin, stem weak-spin + version prior, head = 3
translations inherited from the stem via the Morse taper), cup rim-ellipse signal, and a
fundamental-domain search option gated on *verified* symmetry. **(4) Do NOT** adopt SE(3)
diffusion, resurrect local per-keypoint GPU curvature, or ship in-engine ML pose
regression.

* Key Findings

** The information floor is provable and independently confirmed by physics, cup literature, and geometry — continuous-symmetric DOFs cannot be recovered from any single-plane projection by any feature
- Angle 01 (proof): for a homogeneous shape S with rotational symmetry R_a(θ)S = S, rotation is measure-preserving, so the ray-attenuation integral (hence every pixel and every statistic: silhouette mask, interior intensity, gradient magnitude/phase, boundary curvature, MI, learned features) is exactly invariant. Corroborated by D'Isidoro & Ferguson 2018 (cup; http://cmbbe2018.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/pen_cmbbe2018/pdf/WEB_ABSTRACTS/Abstracts_CMBBE2018_50.pdf), Petto et al. 2021 (cylindrical dental implant; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7937219/), Otake et al. 2018 (screws modeled as 5-DoF; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5901722/), TREA patent (extra measurement configurations required; https://trea.com/information/method-and-apparatus-for-determining-a-pose-of-an-implant/patentapplication/ff171a4a-1967-4282-af8f-e5e290035527).
- Angle 02 (cup literature): the field's fix is not a feature — it removes the unobservable DOF from the parameter space (cup ellipse paper, 5-DOF (θ anteversion, φ inclination, k, l, h), spin never enters; https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07763v1); clinical practice measures only inclination + anteversion from the rim ellipse (PMC9813239).
- Angle 05 (geometry): cup/glenosphere = C∞v (spin about polar axis floored); femoral/humeral head = O(3) (all three rotations floored; recoverable only because the head is Morse-tapered to the asymmetric stem); biplane strengthens conditioning but does *not* lift a continuous floor (both views are invariant for an exact hemisphere).
- What it means for "fixing the cost": for continuous-symmetric parts there is nothing to fix — the levers are external reference (D'Isidoro's pelvic-bone route), kinematic/temporal priors, canonical/quotient parameterization with honest covariance, or multi-view. This is a floor, not a signal problem.

** "Near-symmetric implant" is not one problem: discrete mirror vs continuous orbit is the axis that governs every other decision
- The repo's knee tibial tray is Class D (approx Cs, exactly two image-equivalent poses) — a *discrete* ambiguity that SymTrap's dyad machinery solves (angle 02 F2, code-verified; Hermans 2007 ICCV documented the same for sagittal-symmetric knee components: https://mlanthology.org/iccv/2007/hermans2007iccv-statistical/).
- Hip/shoulder classes are structurally different (angle 05 Finding 1): Class A spherical head (O(3), no axis at all), Class B cup/glenosphere (C∞v continuous spin), Class C stems (C1 exact, near-axisymmetric — the *deviation* from axisymmetry is the entire source of spin signal), Class E glenoid (radiographically invisible — all-PE is radiolucent; register bone or markers, never render the STL as a silhouette source).
- Consequences: the dyad engine must NOT be over-generalized to continuous classes (angles 02 F3, 05 Finding 4); "like the knee but rounder" is the wrong mental model (angle 05 Finding 7); and there is no single "symmetric axis" per implant — the pose space should be parameterized per class (angle 05 Implications 6a).
- Oblique-view guidance is a rare cross-angle consensus (angles 02, 05): a single 30–45° oblique monoplane view beats an AP-only view for symmetry-adjacent DOFs and is the only sensor-side change that helps without new algorithms (Jensen 2024's imaging protocol; D'Isidoro 2017 stem IE RMS ~2.0° at 45° oblique vs 1.2–1.4° for other rotations).

** The repo already ships the field's validated pattern for the discrete case (SymTrap = Jensen-2024-style dyad + context prior); the gap is sequence-level disambiguation, not dyad generation
- Code-verified (angle 02 F2): `compute_mirror_pose` implements the Crane-Duffy mirror dyad (axis M = z_ax × view, rotation 2·acos(z_ax·view)); `tibial_pose_selector` implements the tibia-after-femur context prior (choose the dyad member with lower varus/valgus relative to the femur). This matches BOP-Distrib's principle that for exact symmetries the equivalence class is a precomputable transform set applied to any recovered pose (https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17297v2).
- Jensen 2024 (J Biomech 170:112172, PMID 38833908) adds two things the repo lacks: (a) sequence-level disambiguation — a spline through "obviously true" frames reorients ambiguous frames (91.1% accuracy / 94% specificity / 67% sensitivity), and (b) an explicit ambiguity classification. Angle 02 ranks the sequence-level spline as the highest-value new capability; angle 04 confirms it is also the *cheapest real transfer* — Jensen's own spline-only path reaches ~91% with zero labels, and the ML classifiers add points, not a regime change.
- Caveat (angle 05 What-Could-Not-Be-Verified): the "<3.6° apparent varus/valgus ambiguous zone" figure is from the JTML paper (verified in angle 02 via the arXiv full text, https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.00057 — 43/392 tibial blunders, 66% symmetry-attributed) and could not be independently re-confirmed by angle 05; treat 3.6° as JTML-reported, not independently re-verified this run.

** The repo's cost side is provably blind about the symmetry axis, and the knee-side rescue is two-sided intensity + gradient orientation — currently absent from the renderer
- Repo fact (local-recon.org:30-40): all 7 registered cost variants are silhouette/contour terms — EVERY image term is invariant under rotation about the implant's symmetry axis; the only rotation-sensitive term is sym_trap_function's varus/valgus penalty (a geometric prior, not an image feature).
- Angle 01 Finding 2: the knee femoral component has NO exact continuous symmetry — all 6 DOF are theoretically recoverable; varus/valgus and depth are ill-conditioned (first-order-flat basins; Yamazaki 2004: "less pointy" depth curve, https://europepmc.org/article/MED/15147013; repo z_profile measures the 6.31 mm depth gap).
- Rescue features in evidence order: gradient orientation (De Silva 2016: 0% gross failures unmasked where gradient correlation failed >14% under implant content mismatch, https://beta.iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0031-9155/61/8/3009/pdf), gradient correlation (Penney 1998, https://doi.org/10.1109/42.730403), pattern intensity (Weese 1999 SPIE 3661; robust to structures present in only one image — the bone/cement content-mismatch situation), two-sided Mahfouz-style direct image comparison (Mahfouz 2003/2005).
- Two blockers in-repo (angle 01 Finding 3, verified): the renderer emits flat-fill 255 with no thickness/attenuation target (render_engine.cu FillTriangleKernel) — two-sided comparison is impossible today; and DIRECT_MAHFOUZ's "intensity" term is one-sided (mean fluoroscopy over template-white pixels — invariant to any rotation that preserves the silhouette). Both are contained changes: a min-z/attenuation accumulation in the existing fragment-fill pipeline + a new comparison kernel.

** The optimizer is in the literature's worst regime for symmetric problems, and the cheapest symmetry-targeted fix costs zero evaluations
- Angle 03 F1: a symmetric cost makes the ambiguity structural — even a perfect cost renders symmetric poses identically. DIRECT's documented reaction is POH explosion + budget spread across the equal-cost orbit (Stripinis & Paulavičius 2022, verbatim: symmetric problems yield "many POHs with the same diameter δ and objective value, leading to a drastic increase of selected POHs per iteration"; https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2109.14912; Gablonsky 2001 Lemma 4.4 + Theorem 4.1 contrapositive, https://repository.lib.ncsu.edu/items/cfdebcfc-8f51-4dbe-a7b8-9634a9b0f9a5).
- Angle 03 F2: tie restriction (one POH per (size, value) group — the IO/Gablonsky lineage) is the literature's documented fix; Jones & Martins 2021: "Best results are achieved by ignoring ties" (https://websites.umich.edu/~mdolaboratory/pdf/Jones2020a.pdf). JTML's config (tie=All, ε=0) is the literature's worst regime for exactly this failure mode (prior-run verification, angle 03 E14). Cost of the fix: 0 extra evals.
- Angle 03 F3/F6: GL two-step Pareto + gb phase (plan 010's 1-DTC-GL-gb) keep both orbit members explored (1-DTC-GL 24,119 evals on perturbed Rastrigin 5D vs 1-DTC-IO 567,269 — GL is the periodic-misalignment insurance), but GL alone returns ONE point; hypothesis retention from DIRECT's own stored corpus is FREE (0 GPU evals; DIRECT never forgets sampled points — Pošík 2009) and is the missing "keep all symmetric hypotheses alive" mechanism, handing N hypotheses to angle-02-style disambiguation.
- Angle 03 F5: fundamental-domain search (SymDIRECT pattern, Grbić 2013; shipped in DIRECTGO as glbSolve-sym) costs *fewer* evals but requires a VERIFIED symmetry — gate it on the probe measurement and harness arbitration, never default.

** The curvature story: "tried and failed" is a fact-error — it was never implemented, and the scoped concept (local per-keypoint heatmaps) was the wrong tool
- Angle 01 Finding 4 (repo facts): the GPU curvature metric is a stub returning constant 0.0 (curvature_hausdorf_metric.cu), never registered, never called; the CPU utilities work but are per-keypoint-local and CPU-side. The 2023 "dud" commit is repo lore, not an evaluation; the 2026-08-12 de-scope verdict was about undefined semantics + allocation leak, and the de-scope hygiene item (remove AllocateCurvatureHausdorfScore at DIRECT_DILATION.cpp:42-43 + banner) is still pending.
- Physics: local curvature is a second-order, noise-amplified feature (Worring & Smeulders 1992; Kerautret & Lachaud 2008) that is *constant along a circular boundary* — provably blind exactly where the symmetry ambiguity lives (angle 01 Finding 4). A globally aggregated contour-shape term (CSS-style) is the only defensible version and has no published implant-2D-3D precedent (absence of evidence).
- Verdict: do not resurrect the local per-keypoint GPU metric; complete the de-scope; if curvature is ever wanted, it must be a global contour term with defined semantics, as a separate measured experiment (angle 01 Implications 1, 6).

** ML: the NO-GO verdict survives its own paper's data, with a sharper reason — image-domain learning is dead on sim-to-real grounds, not just data-scale grounds
- Regression: the one published deep pose-regression system for knee implants in single-plane fluoroscopy consumed ~106,000 annotated images (Vogl 2022, https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0270596) vs the repo's 3 labeled frames — a different problem class (angle 04 F1).
- Sim-to-real: DeepDRR 2018 (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1803.08606) — naive-DRR-trained models FAIL on clinical C-arm data; realistic simulation transfers without adaptation; metal explicitly unmodeled. SyntheX 2024 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10959504/) — realistic sim + domain randomization ≈ real-data training on hip fluoroscopy (7.59 vs 6.90 mm landmark error); naive sim degrades. The repo's renderer is CAD-silhouette-grade (naive-DRR-class) and the literature's *realistic* simulators don't even model metal — the exact physics that makes implants hard.
- Same-task comparison (Jensen 2024): the learned edge over the analytic spline/continuity path is a few points, at the cost of ~13,000 human-supervised sequences. The repo's analytic directive already delivers approximately the same accuracy with zero data (angle 04 F2).
- Even SOTA deep pose does not solve discrete symmetry: FoundationPose flips between valid symmetric orientations at test time (GitHub issues #395/#107) — the repo's analytic dyad directive is ahead of FoundationPose on this specific axis (angle 04 F5).
- The one learned experiment worth running is a research-only, measurement-only ablation:
  synthetic-data pose-domain scoring of the dyad/continuity directive (labels known by
  construction, avoiding the loop-closure label problem) — not a shipped component (angle
  04 Implications 2).

* Contradictions and Open Questions

** Whether the repo's dyad machinery transfers to hip/shoulder — convergent "no, not directly," but inference-level, not measured
- Angle 02 F3: "the dyad engine does not transfer directly to hip/shoulder: it must be replaced by canonicalization + orbit reporting" — confidence *medium* (inference from geometry). Angle 05 Finding 4: "angles 02 must not over-generalize the dyad/mirror machinery to continuous-symmetry classes." Angle 01 Finding 5: the classification rule (stabilizer of the projection map = continuous symmetry group) is what matters, not a per-implant dyad. The three angles agree; but no hip/shoulder model exists in the repo to verify the boundary empirically — that is precisely a measurement-apparatus gap (see Declined-Layer Gaps).

** The "SVM-RBF disambiguation" claim in the user's beliefs is unconfirmed
- Both angle 02 (What-Could-Not-Be-Verified) and angle 04 flag it: Jensen 2024's abstract verifies "eight classification machine learning algorithms" at 88–94% accuracy but does not name SVM-RBF, F1, or scikit-learn; the full text is paywalled (PMID 38833908). Treat the mechanism as "8 ML classifiers," not specifically SVM-RBF. The repo's 2026-08-12 verdict (cheap transfer = psi/continuity measurement, not retraining in C++) is unaffected.

** View-dependence: ambiguity is a property of (pose, view), not of the implant — but the numbers transfer uncertainly
- Jensen 2024: classifier accuracy 71% within 5° of pure-lateral vs 88% beyond (VERIFIED abstract). JTML: ambiguous zone at apparent tibial varus/valgus < 3.6°, ~85% of blunders inside (verified via arXiv full text in angle 02; angle 05 could not independently re-confirm the 3.6° value). BOP-Distrib: equivalence sets are per-image, precomputable only for exact global symmetries. Consensus: emit ambiguity per frame (dyad cost gap + apparent varus/valgus + view-angle metric) and prefer oblique views. Uncertainty: the repo's exact fixtures and render noise change the boundary — needs the probe instrument to measure, not cite.

** Render-noise tolerance of the orbit symmetry check is unmeasured
- Angle 03 F8 proposes two inline symmetry detections: (a) a behavioral fingerprint at 0 evals (persistent high tie-count of equal-(size, value) POHs — documented signature of symmetric/flat structure), and (b) an explicit orbit check at 1 eval each (|f(x) − f(M(x))| ≈ 0; 300 checks ≈ 43 ms ≈ 0.9% of a 35k budget). Both are literature-mechanism high-confidence but transfer medium-confidence: whether the GPU render cost is *exactly* symmetric under the mirror transform (rasterization, Canny, dilation discretization) is a measurement (angle 03 What-Could-Not-Be-Verified; angle 01 says the deciding number comes from the z_profile-style instrument).

** Whether two-sided intensity/GO actually rescues varus/valgus and IE in THIS renderer
- Angle 01: high confidence in the conditioning diagnosis (Yamazaki abstract + geometry), medium confidence in the degree of rescue achievable here — requires the new thickness/attenuation render target, unmeasured. The R15-safe path is explicit: wire the variant, measure valley depth per weak DOF with jtml.z_profile (extended to rotation sweeps) *before* wiring weights.

** Continuous-floor vs biplane: consistent but worth stating
- Angle 05 (analytic): a biplane pair of an exactly C∞v hemisphere still sees no spin signal — both views are invariant; biplane over-determines the polar axis and strengthens stem-spin conditioning, but does not lift a continuous floor. Angle 02 notes the shoulder field moved to biplane (Zhu 2012) because single-plane is weak — that is conditioning, not floor. No contradiction; the practical consequence is that hip/shoulder single-plane work must either add asymmetric context (pelvic bone, neck, screw holes) or declare DOFs unobservable.

** Thin spots (evidence absent, flagged for the owner)
- No published "ambiguity rate" for cups/stems/glenospheres — the cup spin is structurally unobservable (rate is not the right quantity), and stem-version errors from AP radiographs were not retrieved as verified numbers (angle 02).
- No published work applies diffusion or any learned multi-modal method to monoplane metallic silhouettes (angles 02/04) — absence of evidence, not verified impossibility.
- No published implant-specific 2D-3D registration uses boundary curvature as the similarity feature (angle 01).
- Yamazaki 2004's per-rotation errors are paywalled; only the depth qualitative claim is verified (angle 01).
- Jensen 2024's exact dyad-construction math and its relation to the repo's CalculateSymTrap sweep design are unverified against the paper (angle 02).
- Serial-number-level implant etchings are NOT demonstrated readable on clinical
  fluoroscopy — treat as unavailable for pose work (angle 05 Finding 6).

* Recommendations

All moves land on existing seams (CostFunctionManager per-variant pattern, DirectOptimizer::Options un-stubs, stage-script registry, jtml_domain pure-layer math), with R15 guards (bit-identical defaults, divergence only on non-default, oracle gates Tier-2 IoU > 0.85 / z_profile / jtml.oracle_multistage caps, 51-entry registry golden fixture for any new variant).

** (a) Knee-side fixes — implementable now, zero new data
1. **Tie restriction (TieSelection::One) on DirectOptimizer::Options** — what: select one POH per (size, value) group instead of All. Evidence: Stripinis & Paulavičius 2022 (symmetric-POH-explosion quote), Gablonsky 2001 (one-per-group DIRECT-l), Jones & Martins 2021 (tie-ignoring wins) [angle 03 F2, E1-E3]. Cost: 0 extra evals. Gate: ValidateOptions divergence-on-non-default; oracle gates unchanged. R15 risk: none (default stays All).
2. **Two-sided intensity/GO cost variant** (e.g., DIRECT_MAHFOUZ_T2 + a GO term) — what: (a) thickness/attenuation render target in render_engine.cu (keep flat-fill mode bit-identical for existing variants); (b) comparison kernel (NCC or pattern intensity — pattern intensity is the documented choice for bone/cement content mismatch); (c) GO term with the fluoroscopy gradient map precomputed once per frame; (d) register via the per-variant path (explicit .cpp, CMakeLists entry, CostFunctionManager, 51-entry golden fixture). Evidence: De Silva 2016 (GO 0% gross failures), Penney 1998, Weese 1999, Mahfouz 2003/2005 [angle 01 Findings 2-3, Implications 2-3]. Cost: ~40–70 µs/eval → ~5–6k evals/s (estimated; measure). This is the R15-safe validation path: measurement, not re-derivation.
3. **Ambiguity-as-data output** — what: standardize an ambiguity record on the pose output (dyad cost gap from the sweep, apparent varus/valgus, view-angle-vs-lateral metric). Evidence: angle 02 F6 (ambiguity is view-dependent; JTML ambiguous zone; Jensen oblique protocol). Cost: ~0 (pure data, no new search). Gate: none beyond the pose-record schema.
4. **Sequence-level disambiguation (Jensen-spline analog)** — what: coordinator-level directive on the stage-script registry seam (plan 009): classify frames obviously-true vs potentially-ambiguous; fit a pose spline through obvious frames; reorient ambiguous frames to the spline-consistent dyad member. Evidence: Jensen 2024 (91.1% spline accuracy, 94% specificity) [angles 02, 04]. Cost: pure domain math, headless-testable, no GPU. Gate: R15 headless tests + hegel PBT; this is the highest-value new capability (angle 02).
5. **Complete the curvature de-scope hygiene item** — remove AllocateCurvatureHausdorfScore at DIRECT_DILATION.cpp:42-43 + add the de-scope banner. Evidence: angle 01 Implications 1; local-recon.org:63-64. Cost: trivial.
6. **Hypothesis retention (`retain_hypotheses` + `GetHypotheses()`)** — what: cluster DIRECT's already-stored low-cost corpus (glcCluster-style) into k distinct basins; return top-k {Point6D, cost} for downstream disambiguation. Evidence: Pošík 2009 (DIRECT stores all points), angle 03 F6, E6/E9. Cost: 0 GPU evals; CPU ms. Gate: none on the default path (additive accessor).
7. **GL + gb (plan 010 core, 1-DTC-GL-gb)** — what: implement GL two-step Pareto (union S=G∪L) + canonical gb phase (5 non-improving iterations / security every 5th); do NOT copy DIRECTGO I_DTC_GL.m's inverted-phase bug (documented, angle 03 E14). Evidence: Stripinis & Paulavičius 2022 (perturbed-Rastrigin insurance), GENDIRECT prose [angle 03 F3]. Cost: 0 net evals (−60–72% at n>4). Gate: battery + oracle; variant promotion R9-harness-arbitrated.

** (b) Hip/shoulder-ready machinery — needs models and a probe first
8. **Symmetry-group dispatcher** — what: per-implant-class descriptor {none | discrete dual | SO(2) orbit | SO(3) orbit} in jtml_domain (pure Qt/GPU-free, the planned Rust-interop surface); dispatch discrete → existing compute_mirror_pose + selector; continuous → canonical-frame output + explicit orbit report, NO dyad selection. Evidence: angle 02 F3/F7, angle 05 Finding 1 (taxonomy A–E). Gate: R12 (build the orbit branch only when hip/shoulder models land); blocked by the measurement-apparatus gap (class assignments need verification).
9. **Per-class pose parameterization (orbit canonicalization)** — what: cup = 5-DOF (θ anteversion, φ inclination, translations) + declared-unknown spin reported as covariance; stem = full SE(3) with weak-spin conditioning + version prior (neck anteversion, the repo's sym_trap-penalty pattern generalized); head = 3 translations only (landmark; rotation inherited from the stem via the Morse taper); glenoid = register bone (scapula) or markers, never the PE STL. Evidence: Suh et al. 2025 (5-DOF ellipse, sub-degree MAE, CPU ~1.2–1.4 s) [angles 02, 05]; D'Isidoro 2023 (spin seeded from skin markers in practice); angle 05 Implications 2-4. Gate: models + probe.
10. **Cup rim-ellipse signal** — what: a cheap analytic rim-ellipse term (Liaw method / Suh 2025 differentiable ellipse) for inclination + anteversion — the one strong, low-cost hip signal that IS in the image. Evidence: PMC9813239 (clinical practice), Suh 2025 [angle 05]. Gate: acetabular cup model + probe.
11. **Fundamental-domain search option** (`fundamental_domain`, default unset) — what: half-range/sector bounds on rotation DOFs for *verified* symmetry, orbit reconstructed analytically via the existing dyad geometry. Evidence: SymDIRECT (Grbić 2013), DIRECTGO glbSolve-sym [angle 03 F5]. Cost: negative (≈half the domain). Gate: HIGHEST behavioral risk — require the symmetry check to pass first (angle 03 F8) and keep it harness-arbitrated (R9); never a default.
12. **Oblique-view guidance** — surface "frame in ambiguous zone; oblique view >5° recommended" via the existing relay pattern. Evidence: Jensen 2024 protocol, D'Isidoro 2017 (IE conditioning at 45° oblique) [angles 02, 05]. Cost: ~0.

** (c) Do NOT do (evidence-backed refusals)
- **SE(3) score-based diffusion** (Hsiao 2024 CVPR): RGB-only evidence, no fluoroscopy/metallic-silhouette publication; sampling cost (1000s of denoising steps/sample) ≫ 143 µs/eval; the sampled posterior retains the symmetry-trap modes so dyad/selector logic is still required. Revisit only if a fluoroscopy-likelihood diffusion with viable sampling or a published metallic-silhouette gain appears [angle 02 F5; angle 04].
- **Local per-keypoint GPU curvature**: never implemented; undefined semantics (the 2026 de-scope critique survives); local curvature is blind exactly at circular boundaries [angle 01 Finding 4].
- **In-engine ML pose regression or pixel-domain mirror classification**: dead at N=3 (Vogl 2022: ~106k images); naive-DRR-grade renderer reproduces the documented DeepDRR failure mode; metal unmodeled in even the realistic simulators [angle 04 F1-F4].
- **Naive multi-start / stochastic swaps**: benchmark-loses at 6-DOF low budget; DIRECT-type determinism owns the 10³–10⁴ window JTML lives in [angle 03 F9, E4].
- **Retrofitting the dyad to continuous classes, or rendering a glenoid STL as a silhouette source** [angle 05 Implications 4-5].
- **Symmetry-aware losses / deep pose networks**: training-time machinery for a network
  that does not exist in the runtime seam; FoundationPose demonstrably fails at the exact
  disambiguation the repo already solves analytically [angle 04 F5].

* Declined-Layer Gaps

Two layers proposed for this run were declined by the user; both are flagged here because they gate any hip/shoulder execution:

** Clinical/domain needs for hip & shoulder registration (declined)
- What was never systematically covered: which DOFs are *clinically reportable* for hip/shoulder 2D-3D kinematics — i.e., the output contract the product must meet (which pose components, at what accuracy, for which clinical questions). Angle 05 touches the edges (clinical practice measures cup inclination + anteversion only, never the spin — PMC9813239; stem version is measured radiologically precisely because the neck breaks cylinder symmetry — PubMed 25737512; knee ambiguity rates are the only quantified numbers in the field: 43/392 tibial blunders, 66% symmetry-attributed), but no angle established a requirements table. Why it matters: it defines whether "report the cup spin as unobservable" is acceptable (it appears to be — the field never reports it), which parameters need priors (stem version from planning), and which implants are even worth single-plane registration (glenoid is not; cup rim + pelvic bone is; stem needs oblique views). Recommendation: a short clinical-DOF requirements interview with the owner (or the knee-study lineage in JTML's papers) before scoping hip/shoulder work.

** Measurement apparatus / symmetric-ambiguity oracle (declined)
- The repo has ZERO hip/shoulder/acetabular models anywhere, no independent CT-based
  ground truth, and no symmetry probe. Everything empirical in this synthesis is therefore
  ungated: angle 05's projection-visibility claims (screw holes as silhouette
  through-voids, coating boundaries as shading) are analytic; angle 03's orbit symmetry
  check and its render-noise tolerance are unmeasured; angle 01's budget estimates (~40–70
  µs for intensity+GO) are extrapolations; angle 02's view-dependence boundary (71%-vs-88%
  regime) transfers uncertainly to the repo's fixtures. The concrete instrument is a
  "rotation-profile" sweep — the z_profile pattern generalized to rotation about each
  class's symmetry axis (render silhouette-IoU vs spin angle for each implant class) plus
  a mirror-cost-gap measurement on the existing knee fixture (measure |f(x) − f(M(x))|
  under the shipped dyad). Why it matters: it is the R15-compatible way to gate every
  hip/shoulder recommendation — measurement, not re-derivation — and it is the only thing
  that can decide whether the symmetry-class assignments in angle 05 are true for real
  meshes. Without it, hip/shoulder work would proceed on unverified geometric assumptions.
  Recommendation: acquire one exemplar STL per class (cup, stem, head, glenosphere) and
  implement the rotation-profile sweep before any hip/shoulder cost or pose-space work.

* Where to Dig Deeper — Answering the Owner's Core Question

The owner asked for "different angles" on near-symmetric implants. The single most useful reading pathway, in order:

1. **Disambiguation (why the repo is already on the right track):** Jensen et al. 2024, J Biomech 170:112172 (PMID 38833908) — the field's current state of the art for post-hoc symmetry-trap correction; read it as the completion spec for the repo's SymTrap (sequence-level spline + ambiguity classification). Then Hermans et al. 2007 ICCV (doi:10.1109/ICCV.2007.4409158) — the earliest explicit treatment of the knee dual-pose problem, 20 lines of context for why the repo's dyad exists. The repo's own lineage: Jensen et al. 2023, arXiv:2205.00057 (JTML paper — blunder statistics, ambiguous zone, tibia-after-femur ordering).
2. **Search-side (what to un-stub):** Stripinis & Paulavičius 2022, JGO 88(3) (arXiv:2109.14912) — defines DTC/IO/IA/GL and contains the verbatim symmetric-POH-explosion quote; skim the section on candidate selection for "symmetric problems." Gablonsky 2001 thesis (NCSU) — Lemmas 4.3/4.4 and Theorem 4.1 give the mechanism in one sitting. Jones & Martins 2021 "The DIRECT Algorithm — 25 Years Later" (arXiv:2101.07150) — the ε=0 and tie-ignoring results. These three fully justify the tie-restriction-first recommendation.
3. **Hips (what is and isn't recoverable):** D'Isidoro & Ferguson 2018 (CMBBE abstract PDF — the canonical cup-floor statement, one page), D'Isidoro et al. 2023 (Front Bioeng Biotechnol 11:1095845, PMC10164959 — in-vivo pipeline; cup spin seeded from skin markers; stem IE 2.0° vs 1.2–1.4°), and Suh, Martin & Moyer 2025 (arXiv:2503.07763 — the 5-DOF differentiable cup-ellipse method; the model to emulate for the hip-side pose space). Otake et al. 2018 (PMC5901722) shows the community simply drops symmetric DOFs (5-DoF screws).
4. **Geometry inventory (the map):** angle 05's taxonomy table (Finding 2) — the per-class symmetry group, asymmetry-breaker, and projection-visibility inventory for stem/head/cup/glenosphere/glenoid. Read it before any hip/shoulder design.
5. **Knee signal rescue (what to build next):** De Silva et al. 2016, PMB 61(8):3009 (GO robustness under content mismatch), Penney et al. 1998, IEEE TMI 17(4) (measure comparison), Weese et al. 1999 SPIE 3661 (pattern intensity for exactly the bone/cement mismatch situation), Yamazaki et al. 2004 (depth conditioning), and Mahfouz et al. 2003/2005 (the two-sided measure and the segmentation-variability argument).
6. **Only if learning is ever reconsidered:** SyntheX 2024 (Nature Machine Intelligence,
   PMC10959504) + DeepDRR 2018 (arXiv:1803.08606) — read together: the realism ladder is
   the entire game, and metal is unmodeled in both; Jensen 2024's classifier-vs-spline
   numbers tell you the learned edge is small.

* Per-Angle Summaries

** Angle 1: Signal Engineering
Which projection features break rotational symmetry, and what cannot: proves the
information floor for continuous-symmetric parts (rotation is measure-preserving ⇒ every
DRR statistic invariant), shows the knee's weak DOFs are a conditioning problem rescuable
by two-sided intensity + gradient-orientation features (currently absent: flat-fill
renderer, one-sided Mahfouz term), and delivers the verdict that the GPU curvature metric
was never implemented and local curvature is provably blind at circular boundaries. Full
report: [[file:angles/01-01-signal-engineering.org]]

** Angle 2: Symmetry Disambiguation
The literature decisively supports post-optimization disambiguation over cost redesign
(Jensen 2024 dyads + classifiers + sequence spline; Hermans 2007; BOP-Distrib ε-symmetry);
the repo already ships the analytic dyad + context selector for the knee; the decisive
axis is discrete-dual vs continuous-orbit (dyad meaningless for orbits — the field drops
the DOF via 5-DOF cup parameterization); SE(3) diffusion is a do-not-adopt. Full report:
[[file:angles/02-02-symmetry-disambiguation.org]]

** Angle 3: Optimizer Basins
Symmetry-tolerant DIRECT search: symmetric/flat landscapes cause documented POH explosion
and budget spread (Gablonsky Lemma 4.4; Stripinis & Paulavičius 2022); JTML's tie=All/ε=0
config is the literature's worst regime; the fixes are tie restriction (0 evals), GL+gb
(plan 010), free hypothesis retention from DIRECT's stored corpus, inline symmetry
detection, and fundamental-domain (SymDIRECT) gated on verified symmetry — with a concrete
ordered un-stub list on DirectOptimizer::Options. Full report:
[[file:angles/03-03-optimizer-basins.org]]

** Angle 4: Learning Under the Data Constraint
ML under N=3 labeled frames + synthetic DRRs: full pose regression is dead (~106k images
needed per the literature), the NO-GO verdict for in-engine learning is upheld on
sim-to-real grounds (naive-DRR renderer; metal unmodeled in even realistic simulators),
binary image-domain mirror classification is the wrong regime (image-degenerate near the
trap), Jensen's own numbers show the analytic spline beats/ties learned classifiers — with
one research-only synthetic ablation as the only worthwhile learned experiment. Full
report: [[file:angles/04-04-learning-symmetry.org]]

** Angle 5: Implant Geometry
The taxonomy that governs everything: hips/shoulders are three different symmetry problems
(O(3) spherical head, C∞v cup/glenosphere with spin floored, C1 near-axisymmetric stem
with weakly-conditioned spin), plus the discrete-mirror tibial tray (solved) and the
radiographically invisible all-PE glenoid; a per-class inventory of asymmetry-breaking
features and projection visibility; biplane strengthens conditioning but does not lift a
continuous floor; oblique views are the consensus data-acquisition lever. Full report:
[[file:angles/05-05-implant-geometry.org]]
